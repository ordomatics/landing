The problem

Senegalese think in Wolof. Paperwork demands French.

A language gap

Letters, contracts, invoices and official reports must be in formal French. Most people speak and think in Wolof.

No keyboard, no computer

Without French-writing fluency or a computer, people pay informal scribes per document today.

It's urgent

A malformed document can stall an administrative process for weeks — at a bank, a court, an employer.

The solution

Speak Wolof. Get a properly formatted French document.

Not a chatbot — an AI agent that writes and edits the open document directly from a typed or spoken instruction.

Dictate in Wolof or French

Voice messages are transcribed and translated automatically; Copilot acts on them immediately.

Import & extract

A PDF, scan or photo becomes editable text and tables — ready to correct and export.
